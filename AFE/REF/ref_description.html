Reference block — afe_ref_tr (1.200 V references and 5 µA bias current, xh018)

Transistor-level reference / bias block of the analog section of the USB 2.0 ULPI PHY. It generates the voltage references of the HS transmitter and receiver (TX_VREF, RX_VREF) and the 5 µA reference current TX_I_5U from a trimmable bandgap and one external precision resistor. Netlist: [afe_ref_tr.sp](afe_ref_tr.sp); datasheet: [datasheet/ref_datasheet.md](datasheet/ref_datasheet.md) (Word: datasheet/ref_datasheet.docx); testbench: [sim/tb_ref_tr.py](sim/tb_ref_tr.py). All values below are ngspice simulations with the X-FAB xh018 models (corner tm, 27 °C, VDD33 = 3.3 V, RBIAS = 12.0 kΩ, 10 pF at each output, trim code 1000b) unless stated otherwise.

1. Function and context

The open interface question Q9 / item I9 (DOC/afe_interface_spec.md, AFE_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md) asks who supplies TX_VREF, RX_VREF and the 5 µA: this block. It is enabled by the power sequencer (bias_en of usb_pwr_seq, pin BIAS_EN), which waits BIAS_CYC = 10 µs before it starts the PLL, so every output must be settled within 10 µs.

  • TX_VREF = 1.200 V — reference of the HS current driver of the transceiver (which builds its driver current from it and its internal 12 kΩ RREF); own unity buffer.
  • RX_VREF = 1.200 V — reference of the HS receiver / squelch thresholds; separate buffer, so current pulses drawn by the transmitter do not reach the receiver.
  • TX_I_5U = 5.00 µA — reference current sourced into the load: VREF / RBIAS / 20 with the external 12.0 kΩ 1 % resistor, so the current is as accurate as that resistor and not as the on-chip poly.
  • TRIM[3:0] — 4-bit trim of the bandgap PTAT gain (0.6 % per code, −4.8 … +4.2 %), from the AFE trim register set (usb_afe_trim_regs, proposal for a TRIM_REF field, Q11).
  • BIAS_EN = 0 — everything off (≤ 6 nA at 125 °C), TX_VREF = RX_VREF = 0 V, TX_I_5U = 0.

3. Why this topology

  • Bandgap from the LDO: the core of afe_ldo_tr (dp3 p+/n-well diodes 1× / 8×, op-amp-forced equal branch voltages, 5 µA PTAT units from one PMOS mirror) is already characterised over the xh018 corners; xh018 has no bipolar devices in this kit. For a reference its op-amp is made 4× larger (input pair 40/4, mirror 8/8): the op-amp offset and flicker noise appear at the output multiplied by ≈ 9.4, so they dominate both the accuracy and the noise.
  • Trim by the PTAT tap, not by a gain resistor: the untrimmed spread of a bandgap comes from the V_BE (diode saturation current) spread. Choosing the tap of the PTAT resistor changes only the PTAT term, so the code that puts VBG on target also puts the bandgap back on its zero-TC point; a trimmed part keeps ≈ 30 ppm/K at every corner. The 16:1 mux is a binary tree of NMOS switches into a gate (A1), so it carries no DC current and its on-resistance does not matter.
  • Gain stage A1 instead of a different string: the bandgap gives its natural ≈ 1.187 V; a non-inverting gain of 1.0113 sets the exact 1.200 V and isolates the string from the buffers. A1 has an NMOS common-source output stage: the supply then reaches the output only through current sources, which keeps the PSRR high at high frequency (a PMOS output stage passes VDD33 ripple above its gain-bandwidth).
  • Two separate unity buffers for TX and RX: single-stage 5-transistor OTAs whose dominant pole is the output node, so they are stable with any capacitive load (PM ≥ 80° from 0 to 100 pF) and need no compensation.
  • Current reference with an external resistor and a source follower: an OTA alone driving the RBIAS pin is unstable with the pin capacitance (≥ 20 pF of pad, ESD and board). The NMOS source follower MNF makes the pin a 1 / gm node; the OTA A_I sets the follower's gate (5 pF on that node). The drain current runs through a wide-swing cascode mirror (20 : 1), giving a 900 MΩ output resistance and ≥ 2.8 V compliance.

4. Circuit

4.1 Bandgap, PTAT string and trim mux

  • D1 (1×, 100 µm²) on node a; R1 = 10.8 kΩ and D2 (8×) on node b. The op-amp MA1 / MA2 (PMOS input 40/4, tail MP4 5 µA) with mirror MN1 / MN2 (8/8) drives MN3, which sets the current of the diode-connected MP0 (node pg); MP1–MP4 and MPN copy it (all 8/4). I_PTAT = V_T · ln 8 / R1 ≈ 5 µA. CCB = 4 pF at the op-amp output gives 66° phase margin.
  • MP3 drives the PTAT string: D3 (1×), R2B = 84.04 kΩ, then 15 segments of 1.4325 kΩ (taps t0…t15). The voltage of tap k is VBE(D3) + I_PTAT · (84.04 kΩ + k · 1.4325 kΩ); tap 8 (95.5 kΩ, code 1000b) = 1.1865 V.
  • The 4-bit mux (15 ref_mux2 cells, NMOS 1/0.35 µm, gates at VDD33 levels) selects one tap as vtap. CFL = 6 pF at vtap with the ≈ 90 kΩ string is a 300 kHz low pass for the supply ripple that MP3's drain capacitance couples into the string; it keeps the PSRR at ≥ 38.6 dB at every frequency (minimum near 0.7–0.9 MHz) and costs ≈ 2 µs of settling.
  • MPN / MNB (4/4 diode) give nb, 5 µA, for all NMOS tails and sinks.
  • Start-up: while the string top t15 is below ≈ 0.86 V, MS2 is off, MS3 (1/10, gate en_inb) pulls sg high and the weak MS1 (1/4) pulls pg down into node a, so the loop leaves the zero-current state. In operation MS2 holds sg at ≈ 20 mV; MS3 is long enough that this costs no accuracy.
  • Off: MBG_OFF pulls pg to VDD33, MBG_O1 grounds o1, MSO grounds sg, MNBO grounds nb.

4.2 Gain stage A1 and output buffers

  • A1: PMOS input pair MGA_A / MGA_B (20/2) with a 10 µA tail (MGA_T, 2 × 8/4 from pg), NMOS mirror (4/4), NMOS common-source output MGA_O (8/1) with a 20 µA PMOS load (MGA_S). Miller compensation RZ = 40 kΩ + CC = 1.5 pF. Feedback RF = 2.266 kΩ / RG = 200 kΩ: vrt = 1.01133 · vtap. DC loop gain 84 dB, PM 120°.
  • B_TX, B_RX: ref_ota cells (NMOS pair 10/2, PMOS mirror 8/4, tail 3 × 8/4 = 30 µA) as unity followers. The 30 µA tail gives a 0.3 V/µs slew rate into 100 pF. 2 pF on chip at each output.
  • Off: MGA_OFF grounds o1a, MGA_VO grounds vrt, BT_O / BR_O ground the outputs.

4.3 Current reference, enable and trim inputs

  • A_I (ref_ota, 10 µA) compares vrt with the RBIAS pin and drives the gate gf (CGF = 5 pF) of MNF (20/1), whose source is RBIAS: V(RBIAS) = vrt, I = 1.2 V / 12 kΩ = 100 µA.
  • MNF's drain is the diode node gi of the mirror MPI (4/2 × 20) / MCI (4/1 × 20); MPO / MCO (1 unit) give TX_I_5U = 5 µA. The cascode gate vcas (≈ 1.70 V) comes from MCB (1/3, diode) with a 5 µA sink MCS.
  • Off: MAI_O grounds gf, MGI_O and MCB_O pull gi and vcas to VDD33.
  • BIAS_EN: NMOS MI (4/1) against the weak PMOS MIL (1/40, ≈ 1 µA) and two inverters (en_in, en_inb): threshold ≈ 0.8 V independent of the logic supply. TRIM inputs (ref_lin): the same stage with a 1/80 load and a series NMOS gated by en_in, so the four inputs draw nothing while the block is off and the mux sees code 0000b.

6. Usage notes and limits

  • Program the trim. Untrimmed, the process spread of the diodes moves VREF by −3.4 / +3.3 %; trimmed it stays within −0.43 / +0.24 % over all corners, −40…125 °C and 2.97–3.63 V; the trim code is found at test (measure TX_VREF or V(RBIAS) at 27 °C, pick the nearest code) and stored by the link in the vendor trim register. The simulation picks the best code per corner automatically (--code auto).
  • Loads must be gates. The buffers' output resistance is ≈ 7 kΩ at DC (single-stage OTA, no output-stage gain); a DC load current of ±10 µA moves TX_VREF by ±74 mV. Keep the DC load ≤ ±100 nA (0.06 %), or add a local buffer at a current-consuming user.
  • PSRR around 1 MHz is 38.6–40 dB depending on the corner, set by CFL and A1. The PLL / CDR supply ripple is on VDD18A, not on VDD33; a larger CFL raises the PSRR at the cost of the 10 µs settling budget.
  • Settling: ≤ 8.3 µs over PVT with 10 pF per output; with 100 pF up to 11.4 µs (slew-limited buffers), so BIAS_CYC must then be 12 µs.
  • Current-loop headroom: at ws, 125 °C, 2.97 V the RBIAS loop is at its limit (V(RBIAS) 0.7 % low, TX_I_5U holds 1 % only down to 2.93 V).
  • Mismatch is not in these simulations (no Monte Carlo models used); the offset table of the datasheet gives the sensitivities (1 mV of bandgap op-amp offset → 9.4 mV at VREF), which the trim removes at 27 °C.
  • RBIAS short to GND: 32 µA at TX_I_5U, 0.82 mA supply current (no damage); RBIAS open: TX_I_5U = 0, the voltage references stay correct.
